Calculation notes

Select the old 90% scheme (expires 30 November 2026) or the enhanced 100% scheme (from 1 December 2026). Confirm employer, employee, course and subsidy eligibility before enrolment.

  • Course fees exclude GST: GenAI Training S$1,650; GenAI Workflow Automation and GenAI Content Creation S$1,300 per person.
  • Select 0% for no SSG subsidy, or 50% / 70% for eligible SG/PR participants. SFEC remains subject to separate course and employer eligibility. Foreign participants receive no SSG subsidy and pay the full course fee upfront.
  • At 50% SSG funding, citizens aged 40 and above receive an additional 20% course fee subsidy, capped at 70% total. Enter eligible citizens in “Citizens 40 & above”; all other SG/PR participants, including PRs of any age, belong in “Under 40 / PR”. Foreign participants receive no age subsidy. Age controls are hidden at 0% and 70% funding, with no additional subsidy applied.
  • Changing either count automatically balances the other group. The target scales the citizen 40+ proportion, rounding down to whole people. The target button applies that split. Changing total pax preserves citizens 40+ up to the new total and adds new pax to the baseline group.
  • SFEC covers the selected 90% or 100% of eligible fees after subsidy, capped at the available balance you enter (S$0–S$10,000). Enter unused credit before this training plan; amounts already spent are not included again. Under the old 90% scheme, foreign employees on eligible courses can also use SFEC against their full course fees, excluding GST. Under the upcoming 100% scheme, foreign participants are not eligible.
  • Both groups share one SFEC balance. This estimate allocates credit to SG/PR first, then to foreign participants; actual drawdown follows trainee and claim submission order. The old-scheme SG/PR pax target accounts for the foreign participants already entered. Foreign employee claims must be submitted after course completion.
  • The old 90% scheme reimburses employers after payment: highlighted amounts show net cost after reimbursement; the summary shows the full upfront payment. The new 100% scheme offsets eligible fees upfront.
  • The target rounds up to a whole person. The final person's fee may exceed the remaining credit. Any excess is included in upfront payable.
  • GST is 9% of the full fee before subsidy and is not covered by SFEC. GST recovery depends on your business's eligibility and is not deducted here.
  • Absentee payroll, EIS and other benefits are not included.
