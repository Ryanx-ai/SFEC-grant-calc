Calculation notes & eligibility

This is a planning estimate for the enhanced SFEC from 1 December 2026, assuming a fresh S$10,000 balance and eligible employer-sponsored courses and employees. Confirm company and course eligibility before enrolment.

  • SG/PR subsidy = full course fee × selected 50% or 70% rate. The selected rate assumes eligibility; it is not an eligibility check.
  • SFEC offsets 100% of eligible fees after subsidy, capped at S$10,000 across both courses. Foreign participants receive no subsidy or SFEC in this calculator.
  • GST is 9% of the full course fee before subsidy. Upfront payable = fees after subsidy − SFEC offset + GST. GST remains payable; any input tax recovery depends on the business's eligibility.
  • Course prices are S$1,650 for GenAI Training and S$1,300 for GenAI Workflow Automation, excluding GST. Absentee payroll, EIS and other benefits are not included.
  • When both courses are selected, the chart allocates credit to GenAI Training first, then Workflow Automation. This is for illustration; total credit used is unchanged.
